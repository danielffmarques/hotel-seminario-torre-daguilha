The Hotel Seminário Torre da Aguilha is the ideal place to rest

Customer reception takes place from 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 a.m.

Our seminary is located between Lisbon and Sintra.
Its construction dates back to 1951 and is the result of a missionary collaboration, initially serving only as a Seminary. Over 60 years passed though several valences. In 1972 it opened a spaced for retreats, conferences, holidays or simply breathe in this beautiful oasis. The property is 11 acres and has 54 double rooms with bathroom, restaurant, cafeteria, 10 meeting rooms with capacity for 10 to 300 people, picnic area with barbecue and a church.
